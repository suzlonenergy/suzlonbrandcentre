Integrated for Real-World Needs

Leadership & Legacy

Leadership: Early champions of renewable energy and climate action, our leadership team has over three decades of expertise across the clean energy value chain. This includes Vinod Tanti (Chairman and Managing Director, Suzlon Group), Girish Tanti (Co-Founder & Vice Chairman, Suzlon Group), and Ajay Kapur (Group Chief Executive Officer, Suzlon Group).

The belief that started it all: Tulsi Tanti believed that renewable energy could solve real-world problems while creating lasting value for society. That conviction continues to guide how we think, act, and build energy ecosystems for the new world.
